📊 Approach Comparison

❌ Legacy Approach (2003-2017)

  • Minimum 8 characters
  • Required: Uppercase + Lowercase
  • Required: Numbers + Symbols
  • Regular expiration (90 days)
  • No dictionary words

Result: P@ssw0rd1!, Tr0ub4dor&3
Hard to remember, easy to crack

✅ Modern Approach (2017+)

  • Minimum 12+ characters
  • No complexity requirements
  • Check against breach databases
  • No forced expiration
  • Natural language allowed

Result: "correct horse battery staple"
Easy to remember, hard to crack

🎯 Why This Matters

  • Entropy: "correct horse battery staple" has ~44 bits of entropy
  • Versus: "Tr0ub4dor&3" has only ~28 bits of entropy
  • Memorability: Humans excel at remembering stories and phrases
  • Security: Length provides exponentially more protection than complexity
  • Breach Protection: Real-time checking prevents reuse of compromised passwords

🔬 The Math:

Legacy approach: 8 chars, 4 character sets = 95^8 ≈ 6.6 × 10^15 combinations
Modern approach: 12+ chars, any characters = Even basic 62^12 ≈ 3.2 × 10^21 combinations
That's nearly a million times stronger!
